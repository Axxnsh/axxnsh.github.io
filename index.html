QUALITY GATES

WCAG 2.2 AA Accessibility & QA Checklist

Testable pass/fail checks for storefront implementation.

Rules: Do (Must Enforce)

  • Use semantic tokens (`color.text.primary`), never raw un-tokenized hex.
  • Every component MUST define default, hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading, and error states.
  • Interactive components MUST document keyboard, pointer, and touch behavior.

Rules: Don't (Prohibited)

  • Do NOT allow low-contrast text or hidden focus indicators.
  • Do NOT introduce one-off spacing (`space.1 = 14.62px`, `space.2 = 73.1px`) or font exceptions.
  • Do NOT use ambiguous labels or non-descriptive actions.
